## Installation

# ChinaTaxCalc — China Individual Income Tax & Expat Residency Tax Engine

Production command-line utility and Model Context Protocol (MCP) toolset for **[chinataxcalc.com](https://chinataxcalc.com)**. Accurately calculates Chinese 7-bracket progressive progressive individual income tax (3% to 45%), foreign national non-taxable fringe benefits, and the 183-day worldwide tax residency rule.
